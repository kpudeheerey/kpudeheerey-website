Privacy, in plain words.

KPU De Heerey is an independent community page. This page explains what happens to the things you send us through kpudeheerey.ca.

What we collect

  • What you type into our forms: your name, Instagram handle, email, phone number, business details and messages.
  • Photos, videos and links you choose to send us.
  • Basic technical information our hosting provider records when anyone visits a website, such as IP address and browser type.

How we use it

  • To review and, if it checks out, post what you sent on our Instagram pages, credited or anonymous as you chose.
  • To reply to you about your submission, advertising inquiry or request.
  • To fix mistakes and handle removal requests.

We don't sell your information, and we don't add you to mailing lists.

Where it's stored

Form submissions and files are stored by our website host, Netlify, which may store data outside Canada, including in the United States. We keep submissions only as long as we need them, and delete them on request.

Your choices

  • Ask us what we have from you, or ask us to delete it.
  • Ask us to take down a post you appear in, or one made from your content, using the Corrections & removals form on our homepage.

Contact

Send us a DM on Instagram at @kpu_de_heerey, or use the forms on our homepage.
